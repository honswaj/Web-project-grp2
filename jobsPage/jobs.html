Job 1: Smart Transport Systems Analyst

Full-time · Melbourne, VIC · Indicative salary: AUD $85,000 to $105,000 + superannuation

Reports to: Transport Solutions Manager

The analyst supports the design and evaluation of digital transport systems, using operational data to improve traffic management, public transport coordination and mobility planning.

Responsibilities include:

  • Analyse traffic, fleet and public transport datasets.
  • Develop and implement data-driven solutions to improve transport efficiency.
  • Work with councils and transport operators to identify operational issues.
  • Assist with system testing, data quality checks and project documentation.

Requirements:

  • Degree in transport planning, data science, computer science or a related field.
  • Experience with data analysis tools (e.g., Python, R, SQL).
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Job 2: Smart Energy Data Engineer

Full-time · Melbourne, VIC · Indicative salary: AUD $100,000 to $125,000 + superannuation

Reports to: Digital Infrastructure Lead

The engineer develops data pipelines and monitoring integrations that help councils and energy partners understand energy consumption, monitor infrastructure performance and identify opportunities to improve efficiency.

Responsibilities include:

  • Build and maintain data pipelines for energy monitoring platforms.
  • Integrate data from meters, sensors and infrastructure systems.
  • Validate data quality, reliability and system performance.
  • Document technical designs and collaborate with software and infrastructure teams.

Requirements:

  • Degree in computer science, data engineering or a related field.
  • Practical knowledge of SQL and Python or a comparable programming language.
  • Understanding of APIs, databases and data integration.
  • Strong troubleshooting, documentation and teamwork skills.
